## 📄 Long Description (Simplified)

**RestoHand Staff - Restaurant Management Made Easy**

Transform your restaurant operations with RestoHand Staff, the mobile app designed for restaurant workers. Streamline order management and enhance customer service with our QR code-based system.

**🍽️ Key Features:**

**Order Management**
• View and manage customer orders in real-time
• Handle QR code-based customer orders
• Track order status from kitchen to table
• Cancel or modify orders when needed

**Menu Control**
• Update menu item availability instantly
• Toggle items available/unavailable
• Manage menu categories and pricing

**QR Code Integration**
• Process QR code-based ordering
• Generate digital receipts
• Handle table-based orders efficiently
• Assist customers with mobile ordering

**Staff Features**
• Real-time notifications for new orders
• Coordinate with kitchen and management
• Track service performance
• Communicate with team members

**Payment Processing**
• Handle digital and cash payments
• Generate QR receipts for customers

✓ Easy-to-use interface for busy restaurants
✓ Real-time updates across all devices
✓ Works offline when needed
✓ Bank-level security for transactions
✓ Fast order processing

**Perfect For:**
Waiters, restaurant managers, kitchen staff, and service teams in restaurants, cafes, and food establishments.
