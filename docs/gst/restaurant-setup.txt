## GST Rate Structure for Restaurants

### Primary Rates
Indian restaurants operate under two main GST rates:

| Rate | Description | ITC Eligibility |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| **5%** | Standard for most restaurants | ❌ No ITC |
| **18%** | Premium establishments | ✅ ITC allowed |

---

## Restaurant Categories

### 1. Standalone Restaurants (5% GST)
**GST Rate:** 5%  
**ITC:** Not allowed  
**Applies to:**
- Independent restaurants (AC and Non-AC both)
- Cafes
- Dhabas
- Quick Service Restaurants (QSR)
- Cloud kitchens
- Food courts
- Takeaway services from standalone restaurants

**Configuration:**
```javascript
{
  establishmentType: 'standalone',
  gstRate: 5,
  canClaimITC: false
}
```

---

### 2. Hotel Restaurant - Budget (5% GST)
**GST Rate:** 5%  
**ITC:** Not allowed  
**Applies to:**
- Restaurants within hotels where **room tariff < ₹7,500 per night**

**Configuration:**
```javascript
{
  establishmentType: 'hotel_under_7500',
  gstRate: 5,
  canClaimITC: false
}
```

---

### 3. Hotel Restaurant - Premium (18% GST)
**GST Rate:** 18%  
**ITC:** Allowed  
**Applies to:**
- Restaurants within hotels where **room tariff ≥ ₹7,500 per night**
- Also known as "specified premises"
- Fine dining establishments in luxury hotels
- 5-star hotel restaurants

**Configuration:**
```javascript
{
  establishmentType: 'hotel_above_7500',
  gstRate: 18,
  canClaimITC: true
}
```

---

### 4. Outdoor Catering Services

#### Standalone Outdoor Catering (5% GST)
**GST Rate:** 5%  
**ITC:** Not allowed  
**Applies to:**
- Independent catering services
- Event catering (standalone)

**Configuration:**
```javascript
{
  establishmentType: 'catering_standalone',
  gstRate: 5,
  canClaimITC: false
}
```

#### Premium Hotel Catering (18% GST)
**GST Rate:** 18%  
**ITC:** Allowed  
**Applies to:**
- Catering services within hotels (room tariff ≥ ₹7,500)
- Event catering with venue rental
- Banquet services in premium hotels

**Configuration:**
```javascript
{
  establishmentType: 'catering_premium',
  gstRate: 18,
  canClaimITC: true
}
```

---

### 5. Special Categories

| Service Type | GST Rate | ITC |
|-------------|----------|-----|
| Indian Railways / IRCTC food services | 5% | No |
| Contract canteen/mess in offices/schools | 5% | No |
| Food in trains (cooked onboard or supplied) | 5% | No |

## Input Tax Credit (ITC)

### What is ITC?
Input Tax Credit allows businesses to claim credit for GST paid on business purchases (raw materials, equipment, services) and offset it against their GST liability.

### Who Can Claim ITC?

✅ **Can Claim (18% GST establishments):**
- Premium hotel restaurants (room tariff ≥ ₹7,500)
- Premium outdoor catering services
- Can reduce net GST payment to government

❌ **Cannot Claim (5% GST establishments):**
- Standalone restaurants
- Budget hotel restaurants (room tariff < ₹7,500)
- Standard catering services
- Simpler compliance but no tax credits

### Benefits of ITC
For 18% GST establishments:
- Claim GST paid on raw materials
- Claim GST paid on equipment purchases
- Claim GST paid on business services
- Reduces overall tax liability

## Special Cases & Exceptions

### Composition Scheme
**Important:** Restaurants **CANNOT** use the composition scheme if they:
- Supply food through e-commerce platforms (Zomato/Swiggy)
- Must use regular GST scheme instead

---

## Food Delivery Platforms

### Major Change (Effective January 1, 2022)

**Before 2022:**
- Restaurants charged 5% GST on food
- Platforms charged 18% GST on delivery charges

**After January 1, 2022:**
- **Platforms (Zomato/Swiggy) collect and pay GST**, not restaurants
- Platform collects 5% GST on food value from customer
- Platform pays this GST to government
- Restaurants only handle GST for dine-in/takeaway at their premises

### Exception
Premium hotels (room tariff ≥ ₹7,500) still charge and pay 18% GST themselves, even for online orders.

### Delivery Charges
- Platforms charge **18% GST on delivery fees**
- This is separate from the 5% food GST

### Commission Treatment
- Platform charges commission to restaurant
- Platform invoices restaurant with GST on commission
- This is a separate transaction

## GST Components (CGST, SGST, IGST)

GST is split into different components based on transaction type:

### Intra-State Transactions (Within Same State)
GST is split equally between Central and State:

**For 5% GST:**
- CGST: 2.5%
- SGST: 2.5%
- Total: 5%

**For 18% GST:**
- CGST: 9%
- SGST: 9%
- Total: 18%

### Inter-State Transactions (Different States)
GST is charged as Integrated GST:

**For 5% GST:**
- IGST: 5%

**For 18% GST:**
- IGST: 18%

### Bill Display Example
```
Sub Total:           ₹3,389.50
CGST @ 2.5%:           ₹84.74
SGST @ 2.5%:           ₹84.74
------------------------
Grand Total:         ₹3,559.98
```

## Alcohol & Beverages

### Alcoholic Beverages
- **NOT covered under GST**
- State VAT / Excise Duty applies
- Taxed by individual state governments
- Each state has different VAT rates

### Mixed Bills (Food + Alcohol)
When a bill contains both food and alcohol:
- GST applies to food and non-alcoholic items
- State VAT applies to alcoholic beverages
- Two separate tax calculations on same bill

### Non-Alcoholic Beverages

| Category | GST Rate |
|----------|----------|
| Coffee, Tea | 5% (in restaurants) |
| Soft drinks, Juices | 12% (packaged retail) |
| Carbonated/Caffeinated drinks | 40% (packaged retail) |

**Note:** In restaurants, beverages follow the restaurant's GST rate (5% or 18%), not retail rates.

---

## Service Charge vs GST

### Service Charge
- **NOT a tax**
- Discretionary charge by restaurant
- Restaurant's income
- Separate line item on bill
- Customer can refuse to pay in some cases

### GST
- **Tax collected for government**
- Mandatory
- Restaurant acts as tax collector
- Must be remitted to government

### Bill Example
```
Food Total:          ₹1,000.00
Service Charge (10%):  ₹100.00
Subtotal:            ₹1,100.00
GST @ 5%:               ₹55.00
------------------------
Grand Total:         ₹1,155.00
```

**Note:** GST is calculated on subtotal **including service charge** (if applicable).

## POS System Configuration

### Required Settings

#### 1. Restaurant Type Selection
```typescript
type RestaurantType = 
  | 'standalone'              // 5% GST, no ITC
  | 'hotel_under_7500'        // 5% GST, no ITC  
  | 'hotel_above_7500'        // 18% GST, with ITC
  | 'catering_standalone'     // 5% GST, no ITC
  | 'catering_premium';       // 18% GST, with ITC
```

#### 2. Business Location
```typescript
{
  businessState: string,      // e.g., "Karnataka", "Maharashtra"
  gstin?: string              // Optional 15-digit GSTIN
}
```

#### 3. GST Configuration Object
```typescript
interface GstConfig {
  establishmentType: RestaurantType;
  defaultGstRate: 5 | 18;
  canClaimITC: boolean;
  businessState: string;
  gstin?: string;
}
```

### Example Configurations

**Standalone Restaurant:**
```typescript
{
  establishmentType: 'standalone',
  defaultGstRate: 5,
  canClaimITC: false,
  businessState: 'Karnataka',
  gstin: '29ABCDE1234F1Z5'
}
```

**Premium Hotel Restaurant:**
```typescript
{
  establishmentType: 'hotel_above_7500',
  defaultGstRate: 18,
  canClaimITC: true,
  businessState: 'Maharashtra',
  gstin: '27ABCDE1234F1Z5'
}
```

## GST Rates on Individual Food Items (Retail)

**Note:** These rates apply to **retail/grocery sales**, NOT restaurant dining. Restaurants apply a single rate to the entire bill.

| Category | GST Rate | Examples |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Fresh Items | 0% (Nil) | Fresh vegetables, fruits, meat, fish, eggs, milk (unpackaged) |
| Basic Packaged | 5% | Packaged rice, wheat, milk products, nuts, dried fruits |
| Processed Foods | 5% | Butter, cheese, refined sugar, pasta, bread items |
| Sweets & Confectionery | 5% | Mithai, chocolates (reduced from 18%) |
| Preserved Items | 12% | Jams, jellies, pickles |
| Luxury Beverages | 40% | Pan masala, carbonated drinks, caffeinated beverages |

## Compliance Requirements

### GSTIN Format
- 15 characters
- Format: `StateCode + PAN + EntityCode + CheckDigit`
- Example: `29ABCDE1234F1Z5`
  - `29` = Karnataka state code
  - `ABCDE1234F` = PAN
  - `1` = Entity number
  - `Z` = Entity type
  - `5` = Check digit

### Invoice Requirements
Every tax invoice must contain:
- Restaurant name and address
- GSTIN (if registered)
- Invoice number and date
- Customer details (for B2B)
- Item-wise details
- Subtotal
- GST rate and amount (CGST/SGST or IGST)
- Grand total

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Applying individual food item GST rates in restaurants
2. ❌ Charging GST on alcohol (use State VAT instead)
3. ❌ Calculating GST per item instead of on subtotal
4. ❌ Not splitting GST into CGST/SGST for intra-state
5. ❌ Using composition scheme with online delivery platforms
6. ❌ Treating service charge as GST
7. ❌ Not maintaining separate records for dine-in vs delivery orders

## Glossary

- **CGST:** Central Goods and Services Tax (Central Government's share)
- **SGST:** State Goods and Services Tax (State Government's share)
- **IGST:** Integrated Goods and Services Tax (for inter-state transactions)
- **ITC:** Input Tax Credit (claim on GST paid for business purchases)
- **GSTIN:** GST Identification Number (15-digit unique identifier)
- **HSN:** Harmonized System of Nomenclature (product classification code)
- **E-commerce Operator:** Online platforms like Zomato, Swiggy
- **Specified Premises:** Hotels with room tariff ≥ ₹7,500 per night
