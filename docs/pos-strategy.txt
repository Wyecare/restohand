POS System Go-To-Market Strategy

1. SUBSCRIPTION TIERS
STARTER - ₹699/month
Target: New cloud kitchens, small cafes (single location) Includes: • Single location • Up to 10 tables • Basic analytics dashboard • Email support (24hr response) • AI PDF menu extraction • QR ordering + payments • First month FREE

PROFESSIONAL - ₹1,299/month (RECOMMENDED)
Target: Growing QSRs, established cafes (2-3 locations) Includes: • Multiple locations (up to 3) • Unlimited tables • Advanced analytics & reports • Priority WhatsApp support • Custom branding on receipts • Inventory alerts • All Starter features • First month FREE

ENTERPRISE - ₹2,499/month
Target: Restaurant chains, franchises (4+ locations) Includes: • Unlimited locations • Dedicated account manager • Phone support • Custom integrations on request • Advanced inventory management • Multi-branch analytics • All Professional features • First month FREE

2. PRICING ROLLOUT STRATEGY
Phase 1: First 20 Customers (Months 1-4)
Price: ₹699/month • Lock them at this price FOREVER ("founding member rate") • Get testimonials and case studies • Goal: Prove product works
Phase 2: Next 30 Customers (Months 5-7)
Price: ₹999/month • Market as "early adopter pricing" • Say: "Normal price is ₹1,299, you get ₹999" • Test higher price acceptance • Goal: 50 total customers
Phase 3: Scale (Month 8+)
Launch Full Tiered Pricing • Starter: ₹699 (basic features only) • Professional: ₹1,299 (main offering) • Enterprise: ₹2,499 (custom) • Grandfather existing customers at their rate • Goal: 100+ customers by Month 12

3. BEATING EXISTING POS SYSTEMS
Your Competitive Advantages:
1. ZERO HARDWARE COST    Competitors: ₹15,000-50,000 upfront    You: ₹0  2. AI PDF MENU EXTRACTION    Competitors: Manual entry (2-3 hours)    You: Upload PDF, done in 2 minutes  3. FASTEST SETUP    Competitors: 2-5 days    You: 30 minutes  4. LOWEST PRICE    Petpooja: ₹833/month + hardware    POSist: ₹2,083/month + hardware    DotPe: 2-3% commission    You: ₹699-1,299/month, no hardware
Against "We Already Have a POS":
The Pitch: "That's great - means you understand the value. Quick question: How much did the hardware cost? And monthly fees?  Most of our customers switched because: • No hardware lock-in - use any phone/tablet • Lower monthly cost • AI menu setup (2 min vs 2 hours) • First month free to compare side-by-side  If you're happy with what you have, that's fine. But try us free for 30 days - worst case, you switch back."
Against "Too Expensive":
Break Down the Math: "₹699/month is ₹23/day - less than one order mistake or wasted dish.  Current losses: • Manual order errors: ₹50-100/day • Printer paper: ₹15-25/day • Printer maintenance: ₹500/month  Plus regular POS costs ₹15,000-30,000 upfront. You save that entire cost. First month is free anyway - let's just try it?"

4. TARGET CUSTOMERS (WHO TO APPROACH)
PRIMARY TARGETS (Highest Success Rate):
✓ NEW cloud kitchens (launched in last 6 months)   Why: No existing system, need cheap solution  ✓ Small cafes (20-50 orders/day, 1-2 locations)   Why: Can't afford Petpooja hardware  ✓ Co-working space cafeterias   Why: Tech-savvy customers, QR-friendly  ✓ Food courts (multiple vendors)   Why: Shared seating, QR makes sense  ✓ College/office canteens   Why: Young crowd, high volume, budget-conscious
AVOID (Waste of Time):
✗ Established restaurants with existing POS (happy with it) ✗ Fine dining (need printed bills, formal service) ✗ Very high volume QSRs (need proven reliability) ✗ Restaurants with owners 50+ years old (tech resistance)

5. WEEK-BY-WEEK ACTION PLAN
Week 1-2: Get 3 Pilot Restaurants
• Contact personal network (friends/family restaurants) • Offer: FREE for 3 months, no commitment • Set up system personally • Visit weekly to fix issues • Record video testimonials
Week 3-4: Expand to 10 Pilots
• Walk into 20 cloud kitchens in your area • Visit during off-hours (3-5 PM) • Give 5-minute live demo on your phone • Same offer: FREE for 3 months
Week 5-8: First Paying Customers
• Start charging ₹699/month (first month free) • Use pilot testimonials in pitch • Visit 5 restaurants per day • WhatsApp outreach: 50 messages/day • Goal: 5 paying customers by Week 8
Month 3-6: Scale to 50 Customers
• Increase to ₹999/month for new customers • Launch Facebook/Instagram ads (₹500/day) • Hire commission-based salesperson • Create YouTube demo videos • Join restaurant owner WhatsApp groups • Goal: 50 total customers
Month 6-12: Launch Full Tiers
• Launch Starter/Professional/Enterprise pricing • Most customers choose Professional (₹1,299) • Hire 2nd salesperson • Hire support person • Goal: 100+ customers by Month 12

1. DEMO MUST BE FLAWLESS    Practice 20 times before first pitch  2. SUPPORT IS EVERYTHING    First 30 customers = over-support them    Any downtime = immediate churn  3. SALES IS A NUMBERS GAME    100 conversations → 10 trials → 1 customer    Must talk to 10 restaurants per day  4. GET TESTIMONIALS EARLY    Video > Text    Record on phone, no editing needed  5. DON'T BUILD MORE FEATURES    Fix offline mode, polish demo, then SELL    Features don't matter until you have customers
