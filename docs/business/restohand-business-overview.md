# Restohand Business Overview

## What is Restohand?

Restohand is a comprehensive **restaurant management SaaS platform** that provides end-to-end solutions for restaurants to manage their operations digitally. We offer a complete point-of-sale (POS) system with integrated payment processing, order management, and customer experience tools.

## Core Business Model

### SaaS Restaurant Management Platform
- **Subscription-based service** for restaurants
- **30-day free trial** followed by monthly/annual billing
- **Multi-location support** for restaurant chains
- **Role-based access** for managers, staff, kitchen, and service teams

### Key Value Propositions
1. **Digital Transformation**: Convert traditional restaurants to digital operations
2. **Streamlined Operations**: Unified platform for orders, payments, inventory, and staff
3. **Customer Experience**: QR code ordering, digital menus, seamless payments
4. **Business Intelligence**: Real-time analytics and reporting

## Target Market

### Primary Customers
- **Independent Restaurants** (cafes, quick service, casual dining)
- **Restaurant Chains** (franchise operations, multi-location businesses)
- **Cloud Kitchens** (delivery-only operations)
- **Food Courts** and **Mall Food Outlets**

### Market Segments
- **Small Restaurants**: 1-2 locations, ₹5-20L annual revenue
- **Medium Restaurants**: 3-10 locations, ₹20L-5Cr annual revenue
- **Large Chains**: 10+ locations, ₹5Cr+ annual revenue

## Core Features & Services

### 1. Point of Sale (POS) System
- **Order Management**: Dine-in, takeaway, delivery orders
- **Menu Management**: Digital menus, pricing, availability
- **Table Management**: Table assignments, session tracking
- **Kitchen Display**: Order routing to kitchen stations
- **Staff Management**: Role assignments, shift tracking

### 2. Payment Processing
- **Integrated Payments**: Seamless payment collection
- **Multiple Payment Methods**: UPI, cards, cash, wallets
- **Split Payments**: Group dining, bill splitting
- **Digital Receipts**: Email/SMS receipts, GST invoices

### 3. Customer Experience
- **QR Code Ordering**: Scan-to-order from tables
- **Digital Menus**: Interactive menu with images, descriptions
- **Order Tracking**: Real-time order status updates
- **Customer Feedback**: Ratings and review collection

### 4. Operations Management
- **Inventory Tracking**: Stock management, low-stock alerts
- **Recipe Management**: Ingredient tracking, cost calculation
- **Staff Scheduling**: Shift planning, attendance tracking
- **Reports & Analytics**: Sales reports, performance metrics

### 5. Multi-Language Support
- **Regional Languages**: Hindi, Malayalam, and other Indian languages
- **Localization**: Currency, tax compliance, local preferences

## Technology Stack

### Frontend
- **React TypeScript** for web dashboard
- **Responsive Design** for mobile/tablet POS
- **PWA Support** for offline functionality

### Backend
- **Node.js with NestJS** framework
- **MongoDB** for data storage
- **RESTful APIs** with real-time WebSocket updates
- **Firebase Authentication** for user management

### Infrastructure
- **Cloud-based** deployment for scalability
- **Real-time Synchronization** across devices
- **Offline Capability** for uninterrupted service

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Subscription Revenue
- **Monthly/Annual Plans** based on restaurant size and features
- **Tiered Pricing**: Basic, Professional, Enterprise plans
- **Per-location Pricing** for multi-outlet restaurants

### 2. Transaction Fees
- **Payment Processing**: Small percentage on transactions
- **Value-added Services**: Premium features, integrations

### 3. Setup & Training
- **Onboarding Services**: Restaurant setup, staff training
- **Custom Integrations**: Third-party system connections

## Business Requirements

### Payment Infrastructure
We need a **robust payment solution** that allows:
1. **Customer Payments**: Seamless payment collection from diners
2. **Restaurant Settlements**: Direct payments to restaurant bank accounts
3. **Platform Commission**: Ability to deduct service fees
4. **Multi-payment Methods**: Support for all popular payment options in India
5. **Instant Settlements**: Same-day or instant payouts to restaurants

### Technical Requirements
1. **High Availability**: 99.9% uptime for restaurant operations
2. **Real-time Processing**: Instant order updates across all devices
3. **Scalability**: Handle thousands of concurrent restaurant operations
4. **Security**: PCI-DSS compliance, secure payment processing
5. **Integration Capabilities**: Connect with existing restaurant systems

### Compliance & Legal
1. **GST Compliance**: Automated GST calculation and invoicing
2. **Food Safety**: Integration with FSSAI requirements
3. **Labor Laws**: Staff management compliance
4. **Data Protection**: Customer data privacy and security

### Support Infrastructure
1. **24/7 Customer Support**: For restaurants during operating hours
2. **Technical Support**: System maintenance and troubleshooting
3. **Training Programs**: Staff onboarding and feature education
4. **Documentation**: Comprehensive user guides and API docs

## Growth Strategy

### Phase 1: Local Market Penetration
- Focus on **Kerala market** initially (Malayalam language support)
- Target **small to medium restaurants** (easier adoption)
- Build strong **local partnerships** and word-of-mouth

### Phase 2: Regional Expansion
- Expand to **South Indian markets** (Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh)
- Add **local language support** for each region
- Scale **support infrastructure** for multiple markets

### Phase 3: National Expansion
- **Pan-India operations** with multi-language support
- **Enterprise features** for large restaurant chains
- **Marketplace integrations** with food delivery platforms
