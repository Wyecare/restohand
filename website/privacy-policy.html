Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 31, 2026

Last Updated: January 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Restohand ("Restohand," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Restohand restaurant management platform, including our staff mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

The Restohand platform provides comprehensive restaurant management solutions including QR code-based customer ordering, menu management, staff coordination, inventory tracking, and payment processing.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, role (waiter, chef, manager), restaurant affiliation
  • Work-Related Information: Order handling records, performance data related to restaurant operations
  • Communication Data: Messages sent through our platform, customer service interactions

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: App usage patterns, features accessed, time spent in app, interaction with menu items
  • Technical Data: App version, crash reports, basic performance analytics

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Operation: Process orders, manage menu availability, coordinate staff activities, track performance
  • Account Management: Create and maintain your staff account, authenticate access, manage permissions
  • Communication: Send work-related notifications, updates about orders, schedule changes, important announcements
  • Analytics: Improve app functionality, analyze usage patterns, optimize restaurant operations
  • Compliance: Meet legal obligations, prevent fraud, ensure security
  • Customer Service: Provide technical support, respond to inquiries

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 With Your Restaurant

We share your work-related information with your employing restaurant including performance metrics, order handling data, attendance records, and other job-relevant information.

4.2 Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our platform:

  • Cloud hosting providers (Google Cloud Platform)
  • Analytics services (Firebase for app improvement and crash reporting)
  • Customer support platforms

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect our rights, prevent fraud, or ensure safety.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication systems
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access controls based on job functions
  • Secure cloud infrastructure with Google Cloud Platform

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations:

  • Account information: Retained while you're employed and for 2 years after account deletion for legal compliance
  • Work performance data: Retained for 1 year for management purposes
  • Order and transaction records: Retained for 3 years for accounting purposes
  • Technical logs: Retained for 90 days for security and troubleshooting

6.1 Account Deletion Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data. To request account deletion:

  • Visit our Account Deletion Request page
  • Fill out the deletion request form with your email and reason
  • Your restaurant administrator will review and approve the request
  • You will receive confirmation within 5-7 business days
  • If approved, your account will be permanently deleted within 30 days

Important: Some data may be retained for legal compliance purposes even after account deletion, including transaction records required for tax and accounting purposes.

7. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For app analytics and crash reporting
  • Google Cloud: For data storage and computing services

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes through the app or email. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
