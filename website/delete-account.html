Delete Account Request

If you wish to delete your Restohand staff account, please fill out the form below. Your request will be reviewed by your restaurant administrator.

Important: Account deletion will remove all your work history, performance data, and access to the Restohand platform. This action cannot be undone.

What happens next?

  • • Your request will be forwarded to your restaurant administrator
  • • The administrator will review and approve/deny the request
  • • You will receive an email confirmation within 5-7 business days
  • • If approved, your account will be permanently deleted within 30 days
